Threefold Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 13, 2026

Threefold ("the app") is a merge puzzle game. This policy explains what the app does, and does not do, with your information. In short: Threefold keeps your game progress on your device. We run no servers of our own and never see your personal data. The app shows ads through Google AdMob to help pay for free downloads, and this policy explains exactly what that involves.

Data we collect ourselves

None. Threefold has no accounts or servers of its own. Your settings, board progress, high scores, unlocked themes, Arcade stars, and any Journal keepsakes you save are stored only in the app's private storage on your device. We (the developer) never receive this information. It leaves your device only if you choose to sign in with Google Play Games (see below), and even then it is Google, not us, that receives it.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

Threefold shows ads served by Google AdMob, a third-party advertising service. Ads appear in two ways, both described here so there is no surprise: six rewarded ad placements you always choose to watch in exchange for something in the game (continuing after a loss, an extra undo, a hint, a coin boost, a theme trial, or an audio-library trial), and one interstitial ad that can appear after a run ends. You can remove every ad permanently with the one-time Remove Ads purchase.

To serve and measure these ads, AdMob and its partners can collect and process:

  • Your device's advertising identifier (a resettable id Android assigns for interest-based advertising, not tied to your name).
  • Other device identifiers and technical signals (device model, operating system version, network type, and similar data used for ad delivery and fraud prevention).
  • Approximate, coarse location signals derived from your IP address or network connection, used to serve regionally relevant ads. Threefold does not request or use any device location permission itself; any location signal involved in ad serving comes from Google's own ad infrastructure, not from the app reading your device's GPS.

This data is collected and processed by Google, governed by Google's Privacy Policy and Google's advertising technology policy, not by us. We do not receive your advertising identifier or any of the signals above ourselves; they go directly from your device to Google.

Your consent choices (UMP)

Threefold uses Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) to ask for your consent to personalized advertising where required, including under the EU/UK GDPR and US state privacy laws. If you are in a region where this applies, you will see a consent form the first time ads are ready to show. You can change your choice at any time from Settings > Privacy options inside the app, which reopens the same consent form.

Resetting or opting out of your advertising identifier

You can reset or remove your Android advertising identifier, or opt out of interest-based ads across all apps, at any time, independently of Threefold:

  • Open the Android Settings app.
  • Go to Security & privacy (or Google > Ads on some Android versions).
  • Choose Ads, then Delete advertising ID (older Android versions instead offer Opt out of Ads Personalization).

This is an OS-level setting that applies to every app on your device, not just Threefold, and does not require contacting us.

Data we share

Other than the advertising data described above, which AdMob collects directly from your device rather than us sharing it, we do not sell or share your data, because we do not collect it in the first place. Threefold has no analytics SDK, no crash-reporting SDK, and no third-party tracking beyond the ad serving described above.

Other network access

Threefold plays fully offline, and outside of ad serving, the only components that talk to anything beyond your device are two further Google Play features, each optional or purely transactional:

  • Google Play Games Services is entirely optional. If you are already signed in to Play Games on your device, the app attempts a silent sign-in on launch. It never shows its own login screen and never blocks play if sign-in fails or you are not signed in; the game works identically either way. If you are signed in, the app syncs your high scores, Arcade stars, unlocked achievements, and Journal keepsakes to your Google Play Games account, so they can follow you to another device and appear on Threefold's leaderboards. You can open Threefold's leaderboards and achievements from Settings, which shows Google's own interface, not a screen of ours. This sync is governed by Google's Privacy Policy, and you control it from your Google Account, not from us.
  • Google Play Billing processes the app's purchases, including Remove Ads. The transaction is handled entirely by Google; Threefold never receives your payment card details, only a confirmation of what you bought.
  • Google Play In-App Review shows the standard Play rating card at a few chosen points during play, never on a fixed schedule. Threefold passes it no information about you or your play, and is not told whether you rated the app or what you said.

Permissions

Threefold's installation package declares the permissions needed for the features above: billing, internet and network-state access (used by Google Play Billing, Google Play Games Services, and AdMob), vibration (the merge/tap haptic), and the advertising-id permissions AdMob itself requires to serve ads. It also declares a notification permission and a boot-restart permission, because of a scheduling library the app bundles for a daily-reminder feature, but that feature is turned off for this release: nothing in the app currently asks you to allow notifications, and no notification is ever sent. None of these entries show you an unexpected permission prompt beyond the standard Android notification prompt on Android 13 and later, which only appears if that dormant feature is ever turned on in a future release.

Your data, in your control

Uninstalling Threefold removes all of its local data from your device. If you signed in with Google Play Games, your cloud-synced scores, achievements, and keepsakes live in your Google account, separate from the app. You can review or remove that data at any time from your Google Account's own Google Play Games settings, or sign out of Play Games to stop the sync going forward. Your advertising identifier is controlled entirely at the OS level, as described above, and is not something we hold or can delete on your behalf, because we never receive it. You can also email us and we will help point you to the right place in your Google Account or device settings.

Delete your data

Threefold has no account of its own and keeps no copy of your data on any server we run, so deleting is something you can do entirely yourself, in two places.

1. Data stored on your device (game progress, best scores, Arcade stars, Journal keepsakes, and every setting including your chosen theme, track and sound layers). To delete all of it:

  1. Open your device Settings.
  2. Go to Apps, then Threefold.
  3. Tap Storage, then Clear storage (or Clear data) to erase it while keeping the app, or tap Uninstall to remove the app and all of its data at once.

2. Data synced to your Google account, which only exists if you chose to sign in with Google Play Games (scores, achievements and cloud saves). This lives in your own Google account rather than with us. To delete it:

  1. Open the Play Games app.
  2. Tap your profile picture, then Settings.
  3. Choose Delete Play Games account & data, then select Threefold to delete only this game's data, or delete everything.

Retention. We retain nothing, because we receive nothing. Local data is gone the moment you clear storage or uninstall. Play Games data is removed on Google's own schedule once you delete it there. Your advertising identifier is held by Google, not by us, and is reset or removed from your device settings as described above.

Children's privacy

Threefold is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children. The app is registered as general audience, not a Families app; if you believe a child has used the app and you have concerns about the ads shown, you can turn on Remove Ads or contact us using the details below.
